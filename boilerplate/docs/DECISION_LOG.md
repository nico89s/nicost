# Decision Log

## Changelog
- **YYYY-MM-DD**: Decision log established.

This log records every decision that changed, contradicted, or refined the original plan: a shift from the initial idea, a flow found broken, or a contradiction between requirements. It is the historical record of **why** the plan changed. It is separate from [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md), which records **what** shipped.
